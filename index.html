Who I Am

A digital identity checkpoint — authentic, curious, and constantly experimenting.

I'm a student and technology enthusiast from India. I learn best by getting my hands dirty: building things, breaking them, and figuring out why they broke. Rather than following tutorials line-by-line, I enjoy pulling systems apart underneath the hood.

Python is my trusty go-to for utilities, bots, and automation. Linux is my personal playground — I have distro-hopped across more installations and custom kernels than I care to admit, and I'm still not entirely sure whether that qualifies as productivity or sophisticated procrastination.

Lately, I've been spending time in the terminal experimenting with AI-assisted coding tools like OpenAI Codex and Claude Code CLI. Somewhere between raw curiosity and actually knowing what I'm doing, you'll find me trying out new ideas and tweaking configs.
